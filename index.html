Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started
What exactly am I supposed to do in POE?

You are a detective in Allantown. Investigate locations, interview people, collect evidence, and follow clues until you can close the case.

Is there a tutorial?

Yes. Select Tutorial from the game’s main menu for instructions on the controls and investigative tools.

Do I need to know Edgar Allan Poe’s stories to play?

No. The cases draw on Poe’s work, but the investigation gives you ways to research relevant information.

Do I need to play the cases in order?

Yes. The campaign progresses from one case to the next.

Can I replay a case after completing it?

You cannot select an earlier case from a completed campaign. Starting a new campaign starts again from Case 1.

Is there a time limit to solve a case?

There is no general real-time countdown for investigations. Follow the clues at your own pace.

How do I know what I’m supposed to investigate next?

Review recent conversations, your notes, the case file, and new places or names that have come up. Use the step-by-step hints if needed.

Interviews and Witnesses
Do I have to type the exact words when questioning someone?

No. Type natural, specific questions about names, places, objects, and events. You do not need to guess one exact sentence.

Why does someone keep giving me the same answer?

Try a more specific question, ask about something else they mentioned, or investigate another lead. Some witnesses have more to say after you learn something new.

Can I ask follow-up questions about something a witness mentioned?

Yes. Follow-up questions are an important part of interviews.

Can I return to interview someone I’ve already spoken to?

Yes. You may need to revisit someone after another discovery.

Can witnesses lie or give me incorrect information?

Do not treat every statement as proof. Compare what you hear with evidence and other accounts.

Does being polite or rude affect conversations?

Pay attention to how people respond. Being polite can matter in some conversations.

Can I show photographs or evidence to witnesses?

Where the interaction supports it, you can use collected photographs or evidence while questioning someone. Watch for the options available at that location.

Locations and Navigation
How do I find a new address?

Search the Police Database for a name, business, or address and add the relevant result to your map. X-PLORE can help you identify businesses and websites first.

What’s the difference between the Police Database and X-PLORE?

X-PLORE is for researching businesses, organizations, and websites. The Police Database provides searchable records and lets you add addresses to your map.

Why doesn’t a particular location appear on my map?

Find and add the address through the Police Database, or follow the relevant clue. Some locations are not available until discovered.

Can I visit locations that aren’t relevant to my case?

Yes. Not every accessible location contains a useful lead.

Why is nobody at a location when I arrive?

Some locations have no one available to interview. Look for other leads or return later if the investigation gives you a reason.

How do I search a room or crime scene?

Click the image to examine possible hotspots. Some places have multiple areas or objects to investigate.

What happens if I remove a location from my map?

Removing a pin clears it from your map. You can search for the address again and add it back.

Evidence and Investigation Tools
How do I collect evidence?

Investigate clickable objects and use the available options to take or photograph relevant items.

What do I do when my inventory is full?

Return to the precinct and place items in the Evidence Locker to free up space.

How do I examine evidence I’ve already collected?

Open the Evidence Locker at your desk and select an item to view it.

What’s the difference between inventory and the Evidence Locker?

Inventory holds items you are carrying. The Evidence Locker stores collected items at the precinct and frees inventory slots.

What is the whiteboard used for?

Use it to arrange evidence and notes and think through connections. It is your workspace, not an automatic puzzle solver.

How do I create or update a suspect dossier?

Open the File Room at your desk and select Dossiers. You can record information and mark people as interviewed, witnesses, or suspects.

How do I take photographs, and what can I do with them?

Use the photograph option at supported locations or interviews. Photographs can become evidence and may be useful during later questioning.

Do I need to collect every piece of evidence to finish a case?

Follow the required investigation and completion steps. You do not necessarily need to explore every optional lead.

Progress, Saving, and Help
Does POE save my progress automatically?

Yes. The game saves as you play.

How do I back up or restore my saved game?

Use the disk icon during play to export a backup. Use Load Game on the main menu to restore a compatible backup.

Can I move my saved game to another computer or browser?

Export a campaign backup and import it with Load Game on the other browser or computer. Keep the backup file.

What happens when I complete a case?

After you close a case, the campaign advances to the next investigation when it is available.

How do I know whether I’ve finished investigating?

When the investigation’s required actions are complete, you can close the case from your desk.

How do I end the case?

Put everything you are carrying into the Evidence Locker and examine any newly added evidence there. Once those tasks and the investigation are complete, the End Case button should appear at the top of your desk.

What should I do if I get completely stuck?

Revisit your notes and recent witnesses, or select your case above to reveal a General Hint, a More Detailed Hint, or the Full Walkthrough for the relevant step.

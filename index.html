Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to use the exact words in interviews?

No. Ask natural, specific questions about names, places, events, and clues. Follow up on what witnesses tell you.

What if a witness has nothing new to say?

Follow other leads, then return after learning something new. Some conversations change as your investigation progresses.

How do I find a location?

Use the Police Database to search for names, businesses, or addresses, then add relevant results to your map. X-PLORE can help you research businesses and their websites.

What if my inventory is full?

Return to the precinct and move collected items into the Evidence Locker to make room.

Can I get help without seeing the complete solution?

Yes. Open the relevant step and reveal the General Hint first. The More Detailed Hint and Full Walkthrough are separate and optional. You can also ask your partner in the game.

How is progress saved?

The game saves as you play. You can also use the disk icon during play to export a backup of your campaign.
